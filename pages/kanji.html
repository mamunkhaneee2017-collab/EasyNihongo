Kanji of the Day

水  みず / スイ

Meaning "water." One of the first Kanji most learners encounter — appears in everyday words like 水曜日 (Wednesday) and 水泳 (swimming).

  • On'yomi: スイ (sui)
  • Kun'yomi: みず (mizu)
  • 4 strokes · JLPT N5
